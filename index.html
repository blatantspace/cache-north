Threat model

Empty land looks unused. That is the mistake.

Distance and cold are infrastructure — but only if people who know them stay ready. Someone else will treat the north as a blank. We do not.

Why cache

A stash is a promise to the next node.

Trapline logic meets machine memory. Gear, signal, skills — held close and spread across the span so no single town or tower is the whole story. If one node goes quiet, another still holds.

Nothing useful stays in one place.

Near-future, field-tested

Sci-fi only counts if it works in weather.

Masks up. Kit that survives −40. Signals that do not depend on one carrier. Faces stay off the feed on purpose — showing skin is a gift to the wrong map.

Organisation

Not a company. A mesh.

DIY cells across northern Canada. Builders and operators who can keep a line honest when the grid does not. Soft public face so the hard work keeps moving.

Cells

Small units. Continuity by trust and kit — not branding.

Caches

Physical and digital stores the next cell can find.

Span

Prairie edge to treeline to ice road. Geography is the chart.
